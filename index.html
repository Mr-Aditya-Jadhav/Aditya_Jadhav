About Me

Engineer at the intersection of platforms & AI

I'm a backend & platform engineer with over 5 years of combined industry and research experience in Java and Python. My work sits at the crossroads of large-scale distributed systems and applied artificial intelligence — building the infrastructure that keeps 1M+ connected vehicles reliably updated and monitored on the road.

At Daimler Truck Innovation Center India, I contributed to architecture of the global xOTA platform — the backbone responsible for delivering over-the-air firmware/Parameters updates to trucks and buses across Daimler Trucks global market. Beyond platform engineering, I built an AWS-hosted RAG-based bug prediction system that reached 80% prediction accuracy and is now embedded in the daily workflow of 50+ validation engineers.

I hold an M.Tech in Computer Science from NIT Calicut (CGPA 8.62/10), where I published research on computational geometry. I'm deeply passionate about developer tooling, AI-augmented workflows, and the future of software-defined vehicles.
