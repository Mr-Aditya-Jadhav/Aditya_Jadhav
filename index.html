About Me

Engineer at the intersection of Automotive & AI

I'm a Software Engineer (Java) with 3.5+ years of industry experience, including 2.5+ years developing embedded Java software for trucks and buses. My work spans over-the-air updates, vehicle diagnostics, GraalVM Native Image, release automation, and private AI/LLM tooling.
